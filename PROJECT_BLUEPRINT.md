# Business Insight Dashboard

## Project Vision

Business Insight Dashboard is a SaaS platform designed for business analysis and decision support.

The main goal is to help businesses collect their operational data, understand their performance, and make better decisions using data-driven insights.

This project is not only a dashboard. The final goal is to build an intelligent decision-support system that can analyze business data and provide recommendations.
